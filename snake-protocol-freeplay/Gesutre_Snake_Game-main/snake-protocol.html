About the Creator

The creator, Leander, developed this project, Jungle Snake Master during the semester exams, transforming study procrastinations into a fun creative exploration. Leveraging MediaPipe’s hand gesture recognition and hosted on GitHub Pages, this game blends cutting-edge technology with timeless arcade thrills made specially for the beautiful and dearest friends of Leander. Many of the game’s distinctive assets were meticously hand-crafted, lending a unique charm to every element. So go on, conquer the jungle, eat foods and claim your new higscores! Dive in and enjoy the challenge!

Features include dynamic power-ups, progressive difficulty, and an immersive gaming experience that combines classic gameplay with modern innovation.
